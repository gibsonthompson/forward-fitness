FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Is Forward Fitness really free?
Yes. Every feature is free: the workout logging, the progressive overload, the progress tracking, and the coach. No subscription, no paywall, no ads, and no card to get started.
Do I need a gym or special equipment?
No. You build your workouts from whatever exercises you want, whether that's a full gym, a pair of dumbbells at home, or bodyweight. The app tracks whatever you actually do.
How does it know what I should lift next?
It saves what you did last time and puts it in front of you on every set. Based on how those sets went, it suggests when to add weight or chase another rep. You always have the final say.
Can I use my own routine or split?
Yes. Set your weekly schedule, build custom workouts, and reorder or swap exercises anytime, even in the middle of a session. Or start from a preset split and make it your own.
What can I ask the coach?
Anything about training, programming, nutrition, recovery, or technique. You get evidence-based answers, and it can look up current research when it helps. For anything medical, it points you to a professional.
Does it work offline?
Yes. You can log your workouts without a connection, so a dead signal in the gym basement is never a problem.
What devices does it work on?
It runs in your browser and installs to your phone or desktop like a normal app, so your workouts and history follow you everywhere.
