Straight Answers.

Still deciding? Here's what people ask before their first workout.

Start Training Free
Is it really free?
Yes. Logging, progressive overload, progress tracking, friends and the coach are all free. No subscription, no ads and no card to start.
Do I need a gym?
No. Build workouts from whatever you have, a full gym, a pair of dumbbells at home or just bodyweight. The app tracks what you actually do.
How does it know what I should lift next?
It saves every set and shows last time's numbers as you train. Based on how those sets went, it suggests adding weight or chasing another rep. You always have the final say.
Can I use my own routine?
Yes. Set your weekly schedule, build custom workouts, and reorder or swap exercises anytime, even mid-session. Or start from a preset split and make it yours.
Who can see my workouts?
Only you, unless you post one. Posts are visible to friends you've accepted, and you choose which stats are included before sharing.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Log your workout with no signal and it syncs when you're back online. A dead zone in the gym basement is never a problem.
What devices does it run on?
Any phone or computer with a browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like a regular app, with your history wherever you sign in.
